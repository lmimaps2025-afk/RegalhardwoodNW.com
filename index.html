What is hardwood floor blending and why do most contractors refuse it?

+
Hardwood floor blending — also called lace-in repair — is the process of integrating new hardwood boards into an existing floor so the seam is invisible. It requires matching wood species, grain direction, plank width, and stain color precisely. Most contractors decline blending because it demands decades of experience to execute correctly, and mistakes are costly to fix. Regal Hardwood Floors has specialized in this work for Portland-area homeowners since 1998.

How much does hardwood floor blending cost in Portland?

+
Blending is priced per project — not per square foot — because complexity varies significantly based on floor age, species rarity, and repair scope. Small lace-in repairs typically start at $1,800. Full blending projects combined with a whole-floor refinish commonly range from $5,000 to $15,000. Every project begins with a free on-site assessment before any pricing is given.

How much does hardwood floor refinishing cost in Portland?

+
Hardwood floor refinishing labor runs $3.40 to $4.60 per square foot depending on floor condition. A typical 1,000 square foot project costs $3,400 to $4,600 in labor. Supply costs are minimal for standard refinishing. Most projects take 3 to 5 days from sanding through final coat. Free in-home estimates for all refinishing work.

Can you match original hardwood floors from a 1920s or 1930s Portland home?

+
Yes — this is one of our specialties. Matching historic hardwood including quartersawn oak, Douglas fir, and narrow-strip maple common in Portland craftsman homes requires sourcing period-appropriate materials, matching plank width and grain direction, and blending stain after sanding both old and new together. This level of detail is what separates Regal from most modern flooring contractors.

Is it better to refinish or replace hardwood floors?

+
In most cases refinishing is significantly better value. Solid hardwood can be refinished 5 to 8 times over its lifetime. Refinishing costs $3–5 per square foot versus $12–20+ per square foot for full replacement. Floors with pet staining, deep scratches, or water damage can often be restored when a less experienced contractor might recommend replacement. We always assess whether refinishing is viable first — because it usually is.

Do you use subcontractors?

+
Never. Every project is completed by Jade Barnes and Justin Barnes directly — no subcontractors, no handoffs. They are a father-and-son team representing the third generation of their family's flooring trade. The person who quotes your job is the person who installs it.

How do you protect the rest of the home during floor sanding?

+
We use professional dust containment — plastic sheeting, sealed doorways, and covered HVAC vents — before sanding begins. We use low-VOC water-based finishes that dry faster and produce significantly fewer fumes than traditional oil-based polyurethane. Most clients can walk on floors within 24 hours of the final coat and move furniture back after 7 days.
