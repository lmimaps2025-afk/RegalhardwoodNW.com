Pricing Questions

What people ask about pricing.

How accurate is the online estimator?

+
Our estimator gives a labor and material range that is typically within 10% of the final quote for standard projects. Final pricing is confirmed at the free in-home walkthrough where Jade assesses floor condition, subfloor, room transitions, and material selection in person. Specialty projects like blending, lace-in repair, and historic restoration cannot be priced online and require an on-site assessment.

Why is your pricing higher than some Portland contractors?

+
Regal is owner-operated — no subcontractors. Every project is completed by Jade or Justin Barnes directly. We use professional dust containment, low-VOC water-based finishes, and take the time required to do the job correctly. Volume contractors charging $2.50–$3.50/sqft typically use subcontracted crews and oil-based finishes. The lower price reflects a different product.

What's included in the refinishing estimate?

+
Refinishing estimates include sanding, staining (if changing color), and three coats of water-based polyurethane finish. Pricing assumes the existing floor is structurally sound. Estimates do not include subfloor repair, stair refinishing, removal of existing flooring, or moving heavy furniture — those are quoted separately if needed.

Do you charge for the in-home estimate?

+
No. In-home estimates are always free for projects in our service area. Jade visits the property, assesses the floor condition, discusses material options, and provides a written quote. There is no obligation to proceed.

Why do you have a 500 sqft minimum for new installs?

+
Setup, sanding equipment, and dust containment for hardwood installation has fixed costs regardless of room size. Small jobs cost the same to set up as larger ones — for projects under 500 sqft, we either bundle them with a larger project or quote them at a higher per-sqft rate that reflects the actual cost. Refinishing has no minimum.
